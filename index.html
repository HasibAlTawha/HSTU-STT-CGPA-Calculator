// behind this project

One student, one late night coffee, and a curriculum worth automating.

This calculator started as a personal tool to track semester results without re-typing 8 course codes every time — and turned into a small project shared with the whole department.

It's kept intentionally simple: no accounts, no servers, no ads. Your data stays in your own browser, and the curriculum stays accurate to the official HSTU Statistics course structure.
